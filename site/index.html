A signal, not a verdict.

Open means active; Draft means not ready for review. Merged is integrated; Closed ended without merging.

Review signals summarize the latest decisive human reviews, not required approvals. “Reported checks passed” only covers checks GitHub reported — it does not prove every required workflow ran. Bot success is not human approval. Open a PR for the full picture.

Not reported means no check result is available. Pending means unfinished; Action required may need a maintainer. Neutral or skipped checks are not passes.
