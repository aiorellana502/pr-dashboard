Read the signals

State: Open — active; Draft — not ready for review; Merged — integrated; Closed — ended without merging. Checks: Not reported — no result available; Pending — awaiting completion; Success — checks passed; Failure — checks failed.

Review: Not reported is not approval. Review requested is not a completed review. Check success, including bot success, does not imply human approval.
